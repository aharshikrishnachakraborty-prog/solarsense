How to read this page

  • Actual vs expected = actual ÷ expected energy. 100% means the plant made exactly what our model estimated for the weather; above 100% means it produced more than estimated.
  • Energy lost counts only detected faults (trips, underperformance…).
  • At risk = energy we could not verify because readings were missing.
  • Use Replay date (top right) to step through the days. It opens on the day with the highest energy loss.

All numbers are computed by the SolarSense pipeline from the Kaggle "Solar Power Generation Data" (two plants in India, May–June 2020).
